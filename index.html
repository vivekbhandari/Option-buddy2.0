Positions

Net price is what you paid or received per share for the whole position (debit = paid, credit = received). For stock strategies it is your share cost basis plus any put bought minus any call sold — shares at 355, sold the 365 call for 3.00 → net 352.00 debit. Value now is optional: the current cost to close the whole position, for the 50%-of-max-profit check.
